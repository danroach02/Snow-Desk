How the pick works

Same inputs serious chasers weigh: snow that falls 4 pm–9 am (first chair) counts most, then half of daytime refills and the model consensus. Snow is computed from each hour's temperature (snow-to-liquid ratio), not a fixed 10:1, so cold, light storms score higher and dense, warm ones lower.

Points come off for gusts over your wind limit (lift holds), rain or mix at the base, road alerts, weekends and holidays (extra for the Cottonwood canyons on powder weekends) and drive time. Alta and Deer Valley drop out when you set Riding to snowboard.
