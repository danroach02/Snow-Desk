How the pick works

Score = overnight snow before first chair (4 pm–9 am) ×10, plus half of daytime snow, plus the model-consensus daily total ×2. Minus gust penalty above your wind limit (lift holds), 15 if rain/mix is likely at the base, 8 for an active road alert, and drive time × your drive weight. Snow is forecast at mid-mountain elevation.
