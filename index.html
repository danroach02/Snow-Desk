How the ranking works

The big number is snow forecast between 4 pm and 9 am, so it is what should be waiting at first chair. Snow that falls during the day is shown separately and counts half as much. The consensus blends four weather models with the NWS forecasters' numbers. Snow is computed hour by hour from temperature (snow-to-liquid ratio), so cold, light storms rank higher than dense, warm ones. Points come off for gusts over your wind limit (lift holds), rain or mix at the base, road alerts, weekends and holidays (extra for the Cottonwood canyons on powder weekends) and drive time. Alta and Deer Valley drop out when Riding is set to snowboard. Tap a resort for measured snow, snowpack, the 7-day outlook and links; tune everything in settings.
